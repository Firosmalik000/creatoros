# Home Surface Brief

Mode: Persuade.

## THESIS

CreatorOS is the managed route from finding the right creator to shipping approved work. A brand or client should understand that promise within the first viewport and move toward creator discovery; creators have a clear secondary path into the curated network.

## OWN-WORLD

The homepage lives inside a Southeast Asian creative agency's working contact sheet: warm paper ground, documentary creator photography, decisive ink typography, small production annotations, and high-energy coral and lime signals. It is neither corporate procurement software nor an open gig bazaar.

## STORY

Open on real creator work and the managed-marketplace proposition. Follow with a labeled illustrative roster, demonstrate the structured discover-to-publish workflow, explain the agency's quality-control role, and close on a concrete invitation to begin discovery.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

At desktop and mobile sizes, show the complete proposition, a genuine three-subject creator contact sheet, and both primary and secondary actions without clipping. Agency management is expressed in the proposition and immediately reinforced by the trust band; no eyebrow label is used.

## FORM

Form seed key: `editorial-talent-contact-sheet`.

The surface uses serif/sans editorial contrast, warm ivory, ink, coral, lime, and cornflower. Public imagery is documentary and authored. Layout favors strong type/image tension rather than generic same-sized feature cards. Navigation, locale switching, and calls to action are keyboard accessible. Indonesian is the default with English and Malaysian Malay alternates. Example talent and metrics are explicitly labeled illustrative, and mobile remains content-first from 320px.
